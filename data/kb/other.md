# General FAQ

## What are your support hours?
Live chat and email support run Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 9 PM IST. Enterprise customers get
24/7 support for critical issues. Messages sent outside these hours are answered the next
business day.

## How do I contact support?
Use the chat icon in the bottom right of the app, or email support from your registered
address. Include your account email and screenshots if possible. Most requests get a first
reply within 4 business hours.

## Where can I check the service status?
Our status page shows live uptime for every component, ongoing incidents and scheduled
maintenance. You can subscribe there to get email or SMS alerts when an incident starts and
when it is resolved.

## Do you have a mobile app?
Yes. The app is available for Android on the Google Play Store and for iPhone and iPad on the
Apple App Store. Sign in with the same account you use on the web.

## How do I share feedback or request a feature?
Go to Help > 'Send Feedback' in the app, or vote on existing ideas on our public roadmap page.
The product team reviews requests every week and updates the roadmap with what is planned.

## How do I report a security vulnerability?
Email our security team with steps to reproduce the issue. We acknowledge reports within 2
business days and ask that you do not disclose the issue publicly until a fix is released. Do
not test against other customers' accounts.

## Do you offer training or onboarding?
Every plan includes self-paced video tutorials in the Help Center. Business and Enterprise
plans also include a live onboarding session for your team, which you can book from Help >
'Book Onboarding'.

## Which languages is the app available in?
The app is available in English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German and Japanese. Change the
language under Account > Preferences. Support is offered in English and Hindi.

## Where can I see what is new in the app?
Release notes are published under Help > 'What is New' and on our blog. Major changes are
also announced by email to workspace admins a week before they go live.

## Where can I find documentation and tutorials?
The Help Center has step-by-step guides, video tutorials and troubleshooting articles. Open it
from Help > 'Help Center' in the app, or search it directly from the help icon.

## How do I join the beta program?
Go to Account > Preferences and turn on 'Early Access'. Beta features are marked with a label
and may change before release. You can leave the beta at any time from the same setting.

## What uptime do you guarantee?
Enterprise plans include a 99.9 percent monthly uptime commitment with service credits if it is
missed. Other plans have no formal guarantee, but we publish our real uptime history on the
status page.
