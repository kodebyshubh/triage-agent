# Billing FAQ

## How do I get a refund?
Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase. Go to Account > Billing > Order History,
select the order, and click 'Request Refund'. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days
back to the original payment method.

## Why was I charged twice?
Duplicate charges usually happen when a payment retries after a temporary bank decline.
Check Account > Billing > Payment History for both charges. If both succeeded, contact
support with both transaction IDs and one will be reversed within 3 business days.

## How do I update my payment method?
Go to Account > Billing > Payment Methods and click 'Add Payment Method'. Set it as
default, then remove the old one. Active subscriptions will bill to the new method on
the next renewal date.

## Can I get an invoice for my purchase?
Yes. Invoices are available under Account > Billing > Order History > 'Download Invoice'
for any completed order, in PDF format.

## What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and RuPay cards, UPI, and PayPal. Enterprise
plans can also pay by bank transfer against an invoice. Prepaid and gift cards are not
accepted for recurring subscriptions.

## Why did my payment fail?
Payments usually fail because the card has expired, the bank flagged the charge, or there
were insufficient funds. Check Account > Billing > Payment History for the decline reason,
update the card under Payment Methods, and click 'Retry Payment'. We also retry
automatically after 24 hours.

## How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to Account > Billing > Subscription and click 'Cancel Subscription'. You keep access until
the end of the current billing period and will not be charged again. Cancelling does not
delete your account or your data.

## Will I be charged when my free trial ends?
Yes, if a payment method is on file. Trials last 14 days and convert to the plan you picked
at signup on day 15. We email a reminder 3 days before. Cancel before the trial ends under
Account > Billing > Subscription to avoid any charge.

## How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Go to Account > Billing > Subscription > 'Change Plan'. Upgrades take effect immediately and
you are charged a prorated amount for the rest of the period. Downgrades take effect at the
next renewal date, and you keep the current plan's features until then.

## Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?
Yes. Go to Account > Billing > Subscription > 'Billing Cycle'. Switching to annual applies a
discount of roughly two months free and starts a new 12-month period today, with unused
monthly time credited. Switching from annual to monthly takes effect at the annual renewal.

## How do I change the billing address or tax details on my invoices?
Go to Account > Billing > Billing Details to edit the company name, billing address and tax
ID (GSTIN or VAT number). Changes apply to future invoices only. To reissue a past invoice
with corrected details, contact support with the invoice number.

## When will I be charged for my renewal?
Subscriptions renew on the same date each month or year as your original purchase. The
next charge date and amount are shown under Account > Billing > Subscription. If that date
does not exist in a given month, renewal happens on the last day of that month.

## Do you charge GST or VAT?
Taxes are added based on your billing address. Customers in India are charged 18 percent GST,
and customers in the EU and UK are charged VAT at the local rate. Businesses that add a valid
GSTIN or VAT number under Billing Details receive tax-compliant invoices.

## Why is my bill higher than usual?
A higher bill usually comes from added team seats, a mid-cycle plan upgrade charged on a
prorated basis, or the end of an introductory discount. Each line item is listed on the
invoice under Account > Billing > Order History.

## Can I get a refund on an annual plan?
Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days we do not offer
partial refunds, but you can turn off auto-renewal so you are not charged for the next year.
Request the refund under Account > Billing > Order History.

## What happens if my payment is past due?
If a renewal payment fails, your account enters a 7-day grace period with full access while
we retry the card. After 7 days, the account is limited to read-only. Paying the outstanding
balance under Account > Billing restores full access immediately.
