# Billing FAQ

## How do I get a refund?
Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase. Go to Account > Billing > Order History,
select the order, and click 'Request Refund'. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days
back to the original payment method.

## Why was I charged twice?
Duplicate charges usually happen when a payment retries after a temporary bank decline.
Check Account > Billing > Payment History for both charges. If both succeeded, contact
support with both transaction IDs and one will be reversed within 3 business days.

## How do I update my payment method?
Go to Account > Billing > Payment Methods and click 'Add Payment Method'. Set it as
default, then remove the old one. Active subscriptions will bill to the new method on
the next renewal date.

## Can I get an invoice for my purchase?
Yes. Invoices are available under Account > Billing > Order History > 'Download Invoice'
for any completed order, in PDF format.
