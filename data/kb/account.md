# Account FAQ

## How do I change my email address?
Go to Account > Profile > Email, enter the new address, and confirm via the verification
link sent to it. Your old email remains active until the new one is verified.

## How do I delete my account?
Go to Account > Settings > 'Delete Account'. This is permanent after a 14-day grace period,
during which you can cancel the deletion by logging back in.

## I forgot my password
Click 'Forgot Password' on the login screen and enter your registered email. A reset link
is valid for 1 hour. If it expires, request a new one.

## Can I merge two accounts?
Account merging isn't self-service. Contact support with both account emails and we'll
manually merge order history and subscriptions within 5 business days.

## How do I update my name or profile details?
Go to Account > Profile and edit your display name, photo, job title or phone number, then
click 'Save'. Changes appear to your teammates within a few minutes.

## How do I add or remove team members?
Workspace admins can go to Account > Team > 'Invite Members' and enter email addresses.
Invites expire after 7 days. To remove someone, open their row in the Team list and click
'Remove'. Their content stays in the workspace. Seats are billed per active member.

## How do I transfer account ownership?
The current owner can go to Account > Team, open the new owner's row, and choose 'Make
Owner'. The new owner must be an existing admin. If the current owner has left the company,
contact support from a verified company email to request the transfer.

## Why is my account suspended?
Accounts are suspended for a payment that stayed past due after the 7-day grace period, or for
a violation of the terms of service. For payment suspensions, paying the balance under
Account > Billing restores access right away. For other suspensions, contact support.

## How do I sign out of all devices?
Go to Account > Security > 'Active Sessions' and click 'Sign Out Everywhere'. This ends every
session except the current one. We recommend changing your password afterwards if you think
someone else had access.

## How do I verify my email address?
Click the verification link in the welcome email. If you cannot find it, go to Account >
Profile and click 'Resend Verification Email'. Links are valid for 24 hours. Some features,
such as inviting teammates, stay locked until the email is verified.

## Why am I not receiving emails from you?
Check your spam or promotions folder first, then add our sending domain to your safe senders
list. Confirm the address under Account > Profile is correct and that email notifications are
on under Account > Notifications. Company mail filters sometimes hold automated emails.

## How do I change my username?
Go to Account > Profile > Username. Usernames can be changed once every 30 days and must be
unique. Old mentions and links using your previous username redirect automatically.

## How do I set up single sign-on (SSO)?
SSO with SAML or Google Workspace is available on the Enterprise plan. An admin can go to
Account > Security > 'Single Sign-On', upload the identity provider metadata, and test the
connection before enforcing SSO for the whole team.

## How do I manage notification preferences?
Go to Account > Notifications to choose which events send email, push or in-app alerts. You
can also set quiet hours, and mute a single project from that project's menu.

## My account is locked after too many login attempts
After 5 failed sign-in attempts, the account is locked for 30 minutes to protect it. Wait for
the lock to expire, or use 'Forgot Password' to reset your password, which unlocks the
account immediately.

## How do I change my language or time zone?
Go to Account > Preferences to pick the display language and time zone. Dates, reminders and
reports use the time zone you choose. Each user sets this separately.
