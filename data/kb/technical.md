# Technical FAQ

## The app won't load / keeps crashing
Try clearing the app cache: Settings > Storage > Clear Cache. If that doesn't help,
uninstall and reinstall the latest version. Persistent crashes should be reported with
your device model and OS version.

## I'm getting a "connection timeout" error
This is usually a network issue between your device and our servers. Check your internet
connection, then try switching between WiFi and mobile data. If the issue persists across
networks, it may be a server-side outage — check the status page.

## How do I reset my two-factor authentication?
Go to Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication > "Reset 2FA". You'll need to verify
via your registered email. If you've lost access to that email, contact support with a
government ID for manual verification.

## My data isn't syncing across devices
Confirm you're signed into the same account on both devices, then force a manual sync via
Settings > Sync > "Sync Now". Sync can take up to 10 minutes for large accounts.
