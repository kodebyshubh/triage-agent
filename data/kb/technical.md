# Technical FAQ

## The app won't load / keeps crashing
Try clearing the app cache: Settings > Storage > Clear Cache. If that doesn't help,
uninstall and reinstall the latest version. Persistent crashes should be reported with
your device model and OS version.

## I'm getting a 'connection timeout' error
This is usually a network issue between your device and our servers. Check your internet
connection, then try switching between WiFi and mobile data. If the issue persists across
networks, it may be a server-side outage — check the status page.

## How do I reset my two-factor authentication?
Go to Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication > 'Reset 2FA'. You'll need to verify
via your registered email. If you've lost access to that email, contact support with a
government ID for manual verification.

## My data isn't syncing across devices
Confirm you're signed into the same account on both devices, then force a manual sync via
Settings > Sync > 'Sync Now'. Sync can take up to 10 minutes for large accounts.

## Which browsers and devices are supported?
The web app supports the latest two versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Mobile apps
need Android 10 or later and iOS 16 or later. Internet Explorer is not supported.

## How do I update the app to the latest version?
On Android, open the Play Store, search for the app and tap 'Update'. On iOS, open the App
Store and go to your profile to see pending updates. The web app updates on its own, so a
page refresh loads the newest version.

## Push notifications are not arriving on my phone
Make sure notifications are allowed for the app in your phone settings and that battery saver
is not restricting it in the background. Then check Account > Notifications in the app. Signing
out and back in re-registers the device for push notifications.

## How do I export my projects?
Open a project, click the menu in the top right, and choose 'Export'. You can export as CSV,
PDF or JSON. Large exports are prepared in the background and emailed to you as a download
link that stays valid for 7 days.

## The app is running slowly
Slowness is usually caused by a large number of open tabs or browser extensions, or an outdated
app version. Close other tabs, disable extensions to test, and update to the latest version.
Very large projects load faster if you archive old items.

## How do I connect an integration such as Slack or Google Drive?
Go to Settings > Integrations, pick the service and click 'Connect', then sign in to that service
to grant access. Only workspace admins can add integrations. To disconnect, return to the same
page and click 'Remove'.

## How do I generate an API key?
Go to Settings > Developer > 'API Keys' and click 'Create Key'. Copy the key right away, because
it is shown only once. Keys carry your permissions, so never share them. Revoke a key from the
same page if it is exposed.

## How do I restore an item I deleted?
Deleted items move to Trash and stay there for 30 days. Open Trash from the sidebar, select
the item and click 'Restore'. After 30 days items are permanently removed and cannot be
recovered.

## The login page keeps reloading
This usually means the browser is blocking cookies for our site. Allow cookies for our domain,
clear the site's cookies and cache, and try again. Private browsing windows with strict
tracking protection can cause the same loop.

## How do I switch to dark mode?
Go to Settings > Appearance and choose Light, Dark, or System to follow your device setting.
On mobile, the same option is under Settings > Display.

## I see an error that says something went wrong
A generic error page usually means a temporary problem on our side. Refresh after a minute and
check the status page. If the error keeps happening on the same action, report it with the time
it happened and a screenshot so we can trace it.

## Does the app work offline?
The mobile apps let you view and edit recently opened projects offline. Changes sync
automatically once you reconnect. The web app needs an internet connection.
